Share the right you with the right person

FounderTouch is a phone-style web app. You pick a mode, such as Founder, Investor or Recruiter, and whoever scans your QR or taps your NFC card sees only that page.

    Modes

    Each mode asks only for what it needs

    A mode shows up for visitors once something has been added to it. Empty modes stay hidden, and Share lists them under Add a mode.

    What's inside

    More than a card

    Share

    A flip card with a QR for every mode. Copy or share the link, download the card as an image, write it to an NFC card, and watch the last 7 days of scans.

    Kits and Hire me

    Each mode has its own decks, demos and documents. Hire me holds experience, education, skills, resume and portfolio, with an Open to work status.

    Recruiter

    Post roles, and candidates apply on your page. A signed-in candidate applies in one tap with their profile. Applicants move through New, Shortlisted, Interview, Offer and Rejected.

    Events

    Branded event pages with your colours, logo and cover. Attendees register and get a QR ticket, and you check them in with the phone camera. Roles can be linked to an event.

    Control

    Make any mode need your approval, lock single files, and send people a private link. Booking links from Calendly, Cal.com and Google Calendar open inside the page.

    Wallet

    Save anyone's card to your in-app wallet, any time. Add your own card to Google Wallet once you add your Wallet keys.

    How a scan works

    From a code to a conversation

    1

    Scan or tap

    The visitor scans the QR or taps the NFC card. The scan is counted by mode and by source.

    2

    See that mode's page

    Swipe through Me, Product, Hire me, Docs, Book and Connect. Locked items ask for approval.

    3

    Connect back

    They save your contact, send their details, book a slot or apply for a role. You see it all in Contacts.

    Candidate scans→Open roles→Apply in one tap→Applicant in Contacts→Shortlist, interview, offer
    Run it

    Local in three commands

    npm install
npm run db     # creates the database and a demo account
npm run dev    # http://localhost:4400
    What is real and what is not yet. It is a working prototype on Next.js and SQLite. The NFC writer needs Chrome on Android over HTTPS. Add to Google Wallet needs your own Wallet keys, and Apple Wallet needs a paid developer certificate. Emails are not sent automatically: you send approval and access links yourself.
    Built with Next.js 15, React 19, Prisma and TypeScript. The source is in a private GitHub repository.
